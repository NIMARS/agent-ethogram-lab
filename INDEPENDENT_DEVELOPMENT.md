# Independent Development and Provenance Statement

Status: informational candidate; release review pending

Updated: 2026-10-29

## Purpose

This note explains, at a general and non-confidential level, how Agent Ethogram
Lab is being developed. It is intended to make the public record clearer; it is
not a substitute for source records, legal advice, or independent verification.

## Development

Agent Ethogram Lab is independently initiated and maintained. The human
maintainer sets the project direction, selects what to investigate, reviews
materials, and decides what may be published.

The public repository is a curated publication surface. It is not represented
as the complete development history, and the absence of a working document
from this repository does not imply that the document never existed.

The current overview reflects development of a local prototype and a limited
exploratory pilot. The materials supporting that work are outside this public
candidate. This statement is not an independently reproducible report of the
pilot or an independent validation of its interpretation.

## AI assistance

AI systems have assisted with activities such as:

- drafting and editing text;
- organizing project materials;
- identifying questions and inconsistencies for human review;
- supporting technical exploration.

AI-generated material is not automatically accepted as fact, evidence, or a
project decision. Public statements should be reviewed by the human maintainer
and, where relevant, checked against primary sources or empirical evidence.
The presence of AI assistance does not establish scientific validity.

## Limits of this statement

This document does not:

- claim that the project's ideas or terminology are novel;
- establish authorship, inventorship, ownership, or legal priority;
- certify that every draft is accurate or complete;
- disclose confidential working materials;
- represent professional legal advice.

Claims about research results, if any are published later, must be supported by
the evidence and methods accompanying those results.

## Public record going forward

Public changes should be recorded through normal version-control history.
Material changes to the public scope, status, license, or evidence claims should
be stated explicitly in the relevant document rather than inferred from a
