# Public Project Scope

Status: public documentation candidate; release review pending

Updated: 2026-10-29

## Purpose

This document defines the subject of the public Agent Ethogram Lab materials
and the boundary of the current documentation candidate. It is intentionally narrower
than the full set of questions that may be explored during private research and
development.

## In scope for the public project

- studying observable behavior produced by LLM-based agents under stated
  interaction conditions;
- considering behavior across extended interactions, not only isolated
  responses;
- documenting the context and limitations needed to interpret an observation;
- developing clear, non-anthropomorphic terminology;
- examining how repeatability, measurement uncertainty, and configuration
  changes affect conclusions;
- publishing high-level research framing and selected, reviewed artifacts when
  they are ready.

## Explicit guardrail

The unit of interest is observable system behavior: outputs, actions, and
interaction records available to an evaluator. The project does not infer a
model's subjective experience from those observations.

## Out of scope

The public project does not claim to:

- establish consciousness, emotions, desires, intent, personality, or moral
  status in an LLM;
- diagnose or evaluate the mental state of a person;
- provide medical, psychological, legal, safety, or compliance certification;
- produce a universal score of intelligence, trustworthiness, or
  “human-likeness”;
- guarantee behavior outside the exact conditions of an evaluation;
- report completed experiments that have not actually been performed and
  reviewed;
- establish that any proposed concept or approach is novel.

## Not included in this public baseline

At this stage the repository does not release:

- implementation designs or production systems;
- detailed evaluation instruments or test scenarios;
- datasets, traces, annotations, or unpublished results;
- internal research notes, source archives, or working discussions;
- operational security information or confidential material.

Future inclusion of any such artifact requires a separate scientific,
provenance, privacy, security, and licensing review.

## Data boundary

No real user or customer conversations are included in this public baseline.
Any future data release must have a documented lawful source, appropriate
permission, privacy review, and clear limitations. Synthetic data must be
identified as synthetic.

## Maturity

Agent Ethogram Lab has a local research prototype and has completed a limited
exploratory pilot with maintainer review. The public candidate remains an
overview of the research direction; implementation, study records, annotations,
and findings are excluded.

Engineering progress and a small pilot do not establish a validated method or
a broadly applicable result. Research questions, measurement choices, and
broader comparisons still require supporting evidence and independent critique.
No hosted service or public software package is offered by this candidate.

## Publication rule

Only files listed in [PUBLIC_MANIFEST.md](PUBLIC_MANIFEST.md) are intended for
this public baseline. The manifest is an allowlist for human review, not a
security guarantee.
