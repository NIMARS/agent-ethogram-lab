# Public Roadmap

Status: public planning draft; release review pending

Updated: 2026-10-29

This roadmap uses evidence and review gates rather than promised dates. It is a
statement of intended sequence, not a delivery commitment.

## Current position

Local development has produced a research prototype and a limited exploratory
pilot with maintainer review. These are internal development milestones. They
do not mark the public gates below as accepted or provide public evidence for
research conclusions.

The next research direction is a separately planned comparison under stated
conditions. Independent evaluation, broader studies, and any hosted service
remain future work. This candidate continues to contain overview documents
only; no software, study records, or findings are being released with it.

## Gate 0 — Safe public baseline

Goal: establish a clean, minimal, and truthful public repository.

- publish only the reviewed manifest allowlist;
- distinguish the development stage from the public contents and license status;
- verify repository history, metadata, and public/private separation;
- keep claims at the level supported by public evidence.

## Gate 1 — Research framing

Goal: determine whether the concept can be stated as a focused research
problem.

- review relevant primary literature and adjacent evaluation work;
- refine terminology and non-anthropomorphic guardrails;
- reduce the candidate questions to a feasible initial scope;
- document unresolved assumptions and limitations.

Exit condition: the public framing distinguishes established prior work,
project proposals, and questions that still require evidence.

## Gate 2 — Study design review

Goal: prepare a reviewable plan before reporting results.

- define the observation unit and comparison conditions;
- identify confounds, uncertainty, and data-governance requirements;
- obtain methodological and domain critique;
- decide which details can be released responsibly.

Exit condition: a limited study can be evaluated for validity, ethics,
provenance, privacy, and reproducibility.

## Gate 3 — Limited pilot

Goal: test feasibility without making broad claims.

The completed exploratory pilot is an initial feasibility step. Its completion
does not by itself satisfy the review and repeatability conditions of this gate.

- use synthetic or otherwise clearly authorized material;
- record the conditions needed to interpret observations;
- examine repeatability and evaluator disagreement;
- report failures and negative findings as well as promising results.

Exit condition: the pilot has a reviewable evidence trail and appropriately
narrow conclusions.

## Gate 4 — Public artifact decision

Goal: decide whether any research artifact is ready for release.

Possible artifacts may include a report, selected examples, or a limited
research tool. Each candidate requires a separate review for:

- scientific support and claim accuracy;
- privacy and security;
- provenance and third-party rights;
- licensing and maintenance expectations;
- misuse and benchmark-contamination risks.

No artifact is promised merely because it appears on this roadmap.

## Gate 5 — External review and replication

Goal: learn whether conclusions remain useful outside the original development
context.

- invite critique from relevant research and technical communities;
- document disagreements and failed replications;
- revise or withdraw unsupported claims;
- expand scope only when evidence and stewardship capacity justify it.

## Cross-cutting rule

A draft is not a result, a result is not a general guarantee, and a public
repository is not evidence that every internal idea or artifact has been
released.
